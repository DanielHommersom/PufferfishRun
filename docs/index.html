Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Pufferfish Run ("we", "our", or "the app") is a one-tap arcade game developed by Digital Diamonds. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

Pufferfish Run does not collect or store any personal information on our own servers.

Through third-party services, the following data may be collected:

  • Device identifiers for advertising purposes (via Google AdMob)
  • Usage data to serve relevant ads (via Google AdMob)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. We do not sell this data to third parties.

All game data, settings, and preferences you create in the app are stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to any server.

We do not collect:

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Health or fitness data
  • Payment information

3. Data Storage

All app data is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's UserDefaults. This includes:

  • Your high score
  • Your selected skin
  • Sound preferences

This data is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Services

Pufferfish Run uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob — to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. Before any personalised ads are shown, you will be asked for permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via Settings → Privacy on your iOS device.

For more information on how Google handles your data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertising and Tracking

Pufferfish Run displays advertisements via Google AdMob. On iOS 14 and later, you will be asked for permission to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. This permission is used to show you more relevant ads.

If you decline tracking permission, you will still see ads but they will not be personalised based on your activity.

You can change your tracking preference at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iOS device.

6. Children's Privacy

Pufferfish Run does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app contains no age-restricted content and is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal data from any user, no special handling for children's data is required.

7. Data Security

Since Pufferfish Run stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit any data to external servers, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Your Rights

Since Pufferfish Run does not collect or store any personal data on external servers, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. All data stored on your device can be removed at any time by uninstalling the app.

With regard to data collected by Google AdMob, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data directly from Google. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for instructions on how to exercise these rights.

If you are located in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not process any personal data, these rights are inherently protected by the design of our app.
