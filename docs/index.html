1. Introduction

Pufferfish Run ("we", "our" or "the app") is a one-tap arcade game made by Digital Diamonds. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used when you play, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device and is not sent to our servers. This includes:

  • High score, coins and game statistics
  • Unlocked and selected skins, hats and trails
  • Daily reward, mission and challenge progress
  • Settings such as sound, vibration, screen shake and language
  • Whether you bought Remove Ads, and your ad consent choice

Deleting the app removes this data. We do not run our own servers and do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a short ad between some games, and optional reward ads you choose to watch (for example to continue a run or to earn free coins).

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before ads are personalised you are asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases — Apple and RevenueCat

You can buy Remove Ads, a one-time purchase that turns off the ads shown between games. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and to restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app (product, date, price, currency and Apple's transaction receipt) and basic device information such as model, OS version and country. It is used only to give you what you paid for. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not ask for an account, email or phone number.

6. Children

Pufferfish Run is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we keep no personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled by:

  • changing your choice in Settings → Privacy options or iOS tracking settings;
  • contacting Google about advertising data, or RevenueCat about purchase data;
  • deleting the app to remove all data stored on your device.

You can always email us and we will help.

8. Data security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Google, Apple and RevenueCat protect the data they process under their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets new features. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
