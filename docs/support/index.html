Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Hold your finger on the screen to inflate the pufferfish and rise. Release to deflate and sink. Navigate through the gaps in the coral without touching the walls or enemies. The longer you survive, the harder it gets.

What is the inflate mechanic?

The more you inflate, the larger your hitbox becomes — making it harder to fit through tight gaps. This is the core risk/reward mechanic of the game. Inflate quickly to rise fast, but stay small enough to squeeze through the coral.

What is the danger ring around the fish?

The danger ring is a visual indicator that appears when your inflate level is high. It pulses faster and turns red as you approach maximum inflation — warning you that your hitbox is at its largest and gaps will be hardest to navigate.

How do I unlock new skins?

Skins are unlocked by reaching score milestones. From the main menu, tap "Select Skin" to see all available skins and the score required to unlock each one. Your progress toward each skin is shown as a bar on the skin card.

My high score is not saving

Your high score is stored locally on your device. Make sure you are not force-closing the app immediately after the game over screen appears — wait for the score panel to fully load before closing. If the issue persists, try restarting the app.

When do ads appear?

A short ad may appear every few deaths. If an ad appears at an unexpected moment, simply close it using the X button and continue playing. Ads help keep the game free.

How do I turn off the sound?

Tap the speaker icon in the bottom right corner of the screen — it is available both on the main menu and during gameplay. Your sound preference is saved automatically.

The game feels too hard — is there a way to adjust difficulty?

Pufferfish Run is designed to be challenging by nature. The difficulty ramps up gradually as your score increases — speed, gap size and enemy frequency all scale over time. Stick with it — most players find their rhythm after a few runs.

What are the enemies and when do they appear?

Three enemy types appear as your score increases:

  • Jellyfish — appears from score 5, moves in a slow sine wave
  • Shark — appears from score 15, charges horizontally
  • Sea Urchin — appears from score 25, bounces diagonally

Avoid touching any enemy or it is game over immediately.

The app crashed or is not working correctly

Try closing the app fully and reopening it. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall the app from the App Store. Your high score is stored locally so it may be reset after reinstalling.
